A Multilingual LLM Framework 
--for Scientific Knowledge Graph Construction 
--from Low-Resource Language Literature

SciKG-LLM, a multilingual framework 
--for scientific knowledge extraction and multilingual knowledge graph construction from low-resource scientific literature. 

Kurdish Medical Corpus (KMC) dataset is preprocessed 
--using Unicode Normalization, 
--Scientific Text Cleaning, and 
--Sliding Window Document Chunking 
--to generate high-quality multilingual inputs.

Ollama framework and performs Few-shot Chain-of-Thought Prompting 
--for scientific triple extraction, 

The extracted knowledge is semantically integrated 
--using LaBSE-based Cross-Lingual Entity Alignment with Cosine Similarity Matching, 

Neo4j Property Graph Construction 
--to create a searchable multilingual scientific knowledge graph.

recall,

knowledge graph quality metrics.
